08 How much do assumptions change the answer?

Method & limits

Four explicitly retained scenarios: forward 20-day blocks; forward 60-day blocks; full history 20-day blocks; forward 20-day blocks with every series’ mean daily log return halved. The half-drift scenario shifts log returns, preserving covariance and observed dispersion. No preferred scenario is chosen from outcomes. Full history incorporates more crises but also development-period selection bias. Five-year drawdown frequency uses running peaks initialized at starting wealth.

10 Borrow & execution cost sensitivity

Method & limits

Charges are applied to the frozen daily holding and turnover path: annual borrow bp × prior-close short notional/NAV ÷252, plus additional execution bp × traded notional/prior NAV. No changed position sizing, cash/guard response, borrow availability, financing interest or cash yield. These are approximate path charges, not funded account reruns. Base trading cost is already 2 bp per side.
