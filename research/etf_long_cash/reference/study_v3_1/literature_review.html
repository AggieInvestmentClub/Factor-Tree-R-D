Interpretation rules

  • Risk-adjusted improvement is different from a higher CAGR; neither establishes causal predictive alpha without appropriate exposure/risk controls.
  • Inverse variance, inverse volatility, leveraged risk normalization and unlevered capped volatility targeting are distinct strategies.
  • A favorable diversified futures or stock-panel result does not imply favorable performance for every single ETF.
  • A classifier's positive sentiment or win probability is not an expected return, calibrated utility or portfolio weight.
  • Papers that motivate a heuristic do not prove that heuristic optimal under this project's different costs and constraints.
  • A 104-matured-week operational floor is not 104 independent observations when outcomes overlap; effective information and estimation uncertainty remain limited.
  • Current-news collection is useful prospectively but cannot manufacture original historical news versions or first-receipt timestamps.
